About The Tool Audit

The Tool Audit is a faceless YouTube channel dedicated to honest, hands-on reviews of AI tools. We don't just read marketing pages — we actually use the tools, test them with real workflows, and show you the results.

Every review follows the same process: we write a script, generate narration with AI, capture screenshots from the tool's website, and build a professional video. The entire pipeline is automated. We even wrote a playbook showing exactly how we do it.

If you're building a faceless channel, running a business that uses AI tools, or just want to know which tools are worth your money — you're in the right place.
